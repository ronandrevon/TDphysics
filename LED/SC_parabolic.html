Physical Insights

Equilibrium ($V_a = 0$): Built-in potential $V_{bi}$ creates an energy barrier ($qV_{bi}$) preventing net carrier flow. $E_F$ is constant across the junction.

Forward Bias ($V_a > 0$): Lowers the potential barrier to $q(V_{bi} - V_a)$. The depletion region shrinks, allowing exponentially more majority carriers to diffuse over the barrier. Minority carriers are injected across the depletion boundaries, driving high forward current.

Reverse Bias ($V_a < 0$): Increases the potential barrier to $q(V_{bi} + |V_a|)$. The depletion width expands, suppressing diffusion. Only a tiny drift current (saturation current $I_0$) flows from thermal generation of minority carriers.
